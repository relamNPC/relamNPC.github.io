Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

Overview

TT Video Uploader ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our tool.

Information We Collect

  • TikTok Authorization Data: We store only the minimum necessary authorization tokens to interact with the TikTok API on your behalf
  • Video Content: Videos you choose to upload are processed locally and sent directly to TikTok's servers

How We Use Information

  • Authorization tokens are used solely to upload your content to TikTok
  • We do not share, sell, or distribute your information to third parties
  • We do not collect analytics or tracking data

Data Storage

  • Authorization credentials are stored locally on your device
  • No data is transmitted to external servers other than TikTok's official API
  • You can revoke access at any time through your TikTok account settings

Data Retention

  • We retain authorization data only as long as necessary for the service to function
  • You may delete all stored data by removing the local configuration files

Third-Party Services

This tool interacts with TikTok's API. Your use of TikTok is subject to TikTok's own privacy policy.

Children's Privacy

This tool is not intended for use by children under 13 years of age.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page.
